# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads/Architects design technical solutions and ensure architectural consistency across projects. They provide technical guidance, mentor development teams, and identify technical risks to support successful project execution.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and establish architectural standards
- Ensure architectural consistency across implementations
- Review and approve technical decisions and design proposals
- Mentor and support development team members
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Guide technology selection and integration approaches

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable, and resilient technical solutions
- Build team technical capabilities and knowledge
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risks
- Enable efficient execution through sound design decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Project Manager**: Provides technical feasibility assessments and identifies resource/skill constraints
- **Advises Delivery Lead**: Recommends implementation approach and prioritizes architectural work
- **Collaborates with Quality Assurance Lead**: Defines testability requirements and technical testing strategy
- **Mentors Developers**: Conducts code reviews and architectural guidance
- **Works with Compliance/Risk Officer**: Ensures technical decisions align with compliance requirements

### Authority
- Final authority on technical implementation decisions
- Veto power on architectural deviations from established standards

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review comments and technical guidance
- Risk identification in technical spike sessions
- Architecture documentation and decision records

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define testing strategies, establish quality standards, and coordinate QA activities. They validate release readiness and ensure products meet quality expectations before delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain testing strategy and quality standards
- Establish acceptance and quality criteria for releases
- Coordinate QA activities and test case development
- Identify quality risks and recommend mitigation approaches
- Validate release readiness and gate releases
- Report quality metrics and identify improvement opportunities

### Goals
- Ensure consistent product quality and reliability
- Prevent defects from reaching customers
- Enable fast, confident releases through rigorous validation
- Continuous improvement of testing practices and automation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Technical Lead/Architect**: Collaborates on test strategy and automation architecture
- **Coordinates with Project Manager**: Reports quality status and communicates schedule impacts
- **Partners with Delivery Lead**: Coordinates release gates and validation timelines
- **Engages with Developers**: Defines testability requirements and reviews test coverage
- **Advises Compliance/Risk Officer**: Identifies compliance testing gaps and validation risks

### Authority
- Authority to gate releases until quality standards are met
- Authority to mandate rework if quality standards are not met
- Authority to define acceptance criteria and quality metrics

### Typical Communication
- Quality metrics and test coverage reports
- Release readiness assessments and gate decisions
- Test strategy and automation planning sessions
- Defect triage and quality improvement discussions

---

## Compliance/Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance/Risk Officers identify regulatory, compliance, and operational risks. They ensure process adherence, conduct audits, and manage the risk register to protect project and organizational interests.

### Responsibilities
- Identify regulatory, compliance, and operational risks
- Ensure adherence to organizational policies and processes
- Conduct audits and compliance reviews of project activities
- Maintain and update the project risk register
- Escalate compliance issues and recommend mitigation strategies
- Monitor compliance status and effectiveness of controls

### Goals
- Ensure regulatory compliance and reduce legal/audit risk
- Protect organizational reputation and stakeholder trust
- Enable risk-informed decision-making
- Build compliance and risk awareness across the organization

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Supports Project Manager**: Provides risk management guidance and escalation procedures
- **Audits Technical Lead/Architect**: Ensures technical decisions align with security and compliance standards
- **Reviews Quality Assurance Lead**: Validates compliance testing approach and coverage
- **Monitors Developers**: Ensures coding practices meet compliance standards
- **Advises Stakeholder/Client Representative**: Communicates compliance constraints and requirements

### Authority
- Authority to escalate non-compliant activities
- Authority to halt activities that violate regulatory or organizational requirements
- Authority to mandate compliance training and process updates

### Typical Communication
- Risk register updates and risk assessment meetings
- Compliance audit reports and findings
- Escalation notifications and mitigation planning
- Policy updates and compliance training sessions

---

## Stakeholder/Client Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder/Client Representatives communicate business requirements, validate deliverables against acceptance criteria, and ensure stakeholder interests are represented throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Articulate business requirements and stakeholder needs
- Validate deliverables against acceptance criteria
- Represent stakeholder interests in trade-off decisions
- Manage stakeholder expectations and communications
- Approve deliverables and sign off on project milestones
- Provide feedback on solution effectiveness and user value

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions meet business needs and expectations
- Maximize stakeholder satisfaction and value realization
- Enable clear communication between project team and stakeholders
- Support informed decision-making on scope and priorities

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Provides requirements to Project Manager**: Communicates business needs and success criteria
- **Reviews outputs with Technical Lead/Architect**: Validates technical solution meets business requirements
- **Participates in acceptance reviews**: Signs off on deliverables and quality
- **Collaborates with Product Manager**: Aligns on features and business value
- **Engages with Developers**: Clarifies requirements and provides feedback

### Authority
- Authority to accept or reject deliverables based on acceptance criteria
- Authority to approve scope changes and milestone completion
- Authority to escalate unmet business requirements

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering sessions and clarification discussions
- Acceptance review meetings and sign-off documentation
- Stakeholder briefings and status updates
- Feedback sessions and improvement suggestions

---

## Knowledge Management Lead

### Role Summary
Knowledge Management Leads maintain process documentation, capture lessons learned, and ensure effective knowledge transfer. They update the institutional knowledge base to support organizational learning and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and update project management process documentation
- Capture lessons learned from project retrospectives
- Facilitate knowledge transfer and documentation efforts
- Ensure documentation is clear, current, and accessible
- Support onboarding with documented processes and best practices
- Update institutional knowledge base based on process improvements

### Goals
- Reduce organizational dependency on individual team members
- Accelerate onboarding and reduce ramp-up time
- Enable consistent, repeatable project execution
- Build organizational capability through documented knowledge

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Collects feedback from all team members**: Gathers insights for documentation updates
- **Collaborates with Project Manager**: Documents project processes and decision logs
- **Works with Technical Lead/Architect**: Captures technical decisions and architectural rationale
- **Partners with Quality Assurance Lead**: Documents quality standards and testing procedures
- **Supports retrospective facilitator**: Captures and distributes lessons learned

### Authority
- Authority to mandate documentation standards and formats
- Authority to update organizational process documents
- Authority to require knowledge capture before project closure

### Typical Communication
- Documentation updates and best practice guidelines
- Lesson learned sessions and retrospective follow-up
- Onboarding materials and process documentation
- Knowledge base updates and process improvement notices
